A Few Common Questions

Do I need to own a home or have pets myself?
No. You don't need any of that. The course walks you through what to expect, whatever your background.
Is house sitting actually free?
Most sits are unpaid. You look after the home and pets, and in exchange, you get a place to stay. Occasionally, a longer sit might ask for a small contribution toward utilities. We cover this honestly in the course.
How do I access the course after I buy?
You get access right away. You'll receive an email with your login details so you can get started immediately.
Is this course self-paced or live sessions?
The course is fully self-paced, so you can work through it whenever suits you.
How long do I have access to the course?
You get lifetime access for a single payment, including any future updates.
Isn't there free stuff on YouTube about house sitting?
There's plenty of inspiration online. This course is the practical path: how to build a profile that gets chosen, write applications that stand out, prepare for a sit, and handle the home and pets with confidence — plus checklists and templates you can reuse. We made it after 22 sits of our own, so you're not starting from someone else's highlight reel.
Who is this for — and who is it not for?
It's for beginners who want to land their first house sit and for travelers who want to stay longer without paying for accommodation every night. You don't need to own a home or have pets. It's not for anyone looking for paid pet-sitting jobs, luxury hotel-style travel, or a "get rich while you travel" pitch. House sitting is unpaid caretaking in exchange for a place to stay — we cover that honestly.
What's your refund policy?
You have a 30-day Ready-to-Apply guarantee. Finish the course. If you still don't feel ready to apply for your first sit, email us within 30 days and we'll refund you in full.
