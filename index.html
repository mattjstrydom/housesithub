Behind the Hub

Meet The Strydom Family

We realized early on that five days in a hotel wasn't enough time to truly know a place. We craved the rhythm of a local neighborhood, the quiet mornings in a foreign kitchen, and the chance to build real relationships.

After navigating 22 house sits across 6 countries—and leaning on 23 years of experience designing educational programs—we realized there was a gap.

We built House Sit Hub to help you skip the guesswork, build a profile owners trust, and step right into the magic of slow, purposeful travel.
