Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Who we are

House Sit Hub runs the Learn How to House Sit course at housesithub.com. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit this site or join the course, and how we use it.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist or enroll in the course, we collect your name and email address through our enrollment form. This information is sent to and stored in our CRM (HubSpot) so we can send you course access details and updates.

When you visit the site, basic analytics data (such as pages viewed and general browsing behavior) is collected through Google Tag Manager and associated analytics tools, so we can understand how the site is used.

Payments

All course payments are processed by Stripe. We do not see or store your full card details. Stripe's own privacy policy governs how they handle your payment information.

Cookies

This site uses cookies for basic analytics and to support the tools listed above. You can control or block cookies through your browser settings, though some parts of the site may not work as intended if you do.

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to deliver the course, send you access details and occasional updates about House Sit Hub, and improve this website. We don't sell your personal information to third parties.

Third parties we use

We rely on a small number of trusted providers to run this site and course: HubSpot (for storing contact details and sending updates), Stripe (for payment processing), and Google Tag Manager and related Google analytics tools (for site analytics).

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to see what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. You can also unsubscribe from any emails using the link included in them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy or your information, contact us at hello@housesithub.com.
